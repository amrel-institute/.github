## About AMREL

AMREL (Applied Malware Research & Education Laboratory) is an independent research initiative focused on malware analysis, reverse engineering, and applied cybersecurity research.

Our work explores practical approaches to understanding malicious software, analyzing binary structures, investigating threat behavior, and developing research-oriented security tools and methodologies.

We aim to bridge theoretical cybersecurity knowledge with practical research through reproducible experiments, technical documentation, and hands-on laboratory work.

## Research Areas

- **Malware Analysis** — Static and dynamic malware analysis, behavioral investigation, and malware classification.
- **Reverse Engineering** — Binary analysis, executable formats, disassembly, and program behavior reconstruction.
- **Threat Intelligence** — Threat data collection, enrichment, correlation, and intelligence-driven analysis.
- **Security Engineering** — Malware analysis automation, analytical pipelines, and research tooling.
- **Applied Research & Education** — Practical cybersecurity experimentation, technical documentation, and knowledge development.

## Research Portfolio

AMREL's research portfolio is organized around applied cybersecurity research, technical investigation, and open-source development.

### Research Domains

**01. Malware Analysis & Reverse Engineering**

Independent research, binary analysis, malware investigation, and reverse engineering.

**02. Open Source Security**

Contributions to open-source security tools, projects, and technical initiatives.

**03. Cybersecurity Research**

Applied security research, threat intelligence, and technical investigations.

**04. Research & Education**

Technical documentation, knowledge sharing, practical experimentation, and cybersecurity education.

Individual projects and publications are reviewed based on their research contributions, provenance, authorship, and ownership before being formally included in the AMREL research portfolio.

## Research Philosophy

AMREL emphasizes:

- Reproducible and evidence-based research
- Technical accuracy and analytical transparency
- Structured experimentation and documentation
- Responsible cybersecurity research
- Continuous learning and knowledge sharing
